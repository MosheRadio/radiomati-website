What we do

Design services and rapid prototype production.

Two halves of one process: designing hardware that solves the problem, then producing it quickly enough to validate the design before the schedule is at risk.

Design services

Four disciplines that usually show up together.

Whichever of these your project needs, it rarely needs just one — which is why we keep them on the same bench.

Design service

Computing & Embedded Systems

Board and software design around modern embedded processors and FPGAs — from schematic and layout through bring-up, drivers and application code.

Design service

Special Environments (Harsh / Medical)

Hardware engineered for demanding conditions — shock, vibration, extreme temperatures, or the constraints of medical-grade design.

Design service

Power & System Engineering

Converter topologies, drivers and full system power architecture, designed to the load rather than to a generic reference design.

Design service

Analog & Control

Signal-chain and closed-loop control design, verified against bench measurement rather than simulation alone.

Design service

Embedded Linux

BSP bring-up, kernel and driver work, and application software layered on the boards we build — one team responsible for both.

Design service

Motion Control

Motor drive stages and positioning systems engineered as a system — driver hardware, feedback and firmware together.

Rapid prototype production

Built on our own line, not a subcontractor's queue.

A prototype is only meaningful if it is built the way production will be. We assemble in-house to keep the two aligned.

Production

SMT Assembly

Fast-turn surface-mount builds on our own pick-and-place and reflow line — first article to small production run.

Production

Through-Hole Assembly

Hand and wave-solder through-hole work for connectors, power components and boards that SMT alone cannot cover.

Production

Wiring & Cable Production

Custom harnesses and cable assemblies built and dressed to your interconnect spec, not a generic off-the-shelf loom.

Production

Electro-Mechanical Production & Assembly

Full box-build — boards, wiring, connectors and enclosure — assembled and checked as one finished unit.

Production

Enclosure & Final Build

Sealed, connectorized units ready to install — not a bare board in an anti-static bag.

Production

First-Article Inspection

Every early build is checked against drawing and specification before it is called a validated prototype.

Next: verification

Once hardware is built, it must still be verified.

EMI testing, environmental testing, on-site diagnostics and failure investigation — review how we confirm a working prototype meets the requirements of a finished product.
