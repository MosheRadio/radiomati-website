What we're good at

Four disciplines, one bench.

Most hardware projects draw on more than one of these at once. We keep them under one roof so design responsibility is never divided between teams.

Power Electronics

Converters, drivers and system-level power design — from concept topology to a board that survives the field.

Analog & Control

Signal-chain and control-loop design backed by real bench measurement — Bode plots, not just simulation.

Motion Control

Motor drive and positioning systems, tuned end-to-end — from the driver stage to the control firmware.

Embedded Linux

BSP bring-up, drivers and application software on the boards we build — one team, one debugger.

EMI and environmental testing, on-site troubleshooting, and failure investigation when something needs an answer.

Where the name comes from

Named after a craftsman whose standard for the work was absolute.

RadioMati is a family company, named after Mati and the radio workshop he ran for decades. The tools have changed — from tube radios to FPGAs — but the standard he set for the work has not.
